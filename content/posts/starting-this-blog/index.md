I have spent more than 13 years keeping production systems running: telecom platforms, IoT cloud, and most recently Kubernetes and AWS platforms at Adidas. Over that time I have written a lot of runbooks, postmortems and design notes. Almost all of them stayed inside company wikis.

This blog is where I will write the parts that are worth sharing outside those wikis.

## What I plan to write about

- **Kubernetes and AWS in practice**: cluster upgrades, networking surprises, cost work, and the setups that held up under real traffic.
- **CI/CD and platform engineering**: pipelines, GitOps, and making the paved road easy to use.
- **Observability and on-call**: alerts that mean something, SLOs, and incident reviews.
- **AI for operations**: what helps an on-call engineer today, and what is still noise.

## How I write

Each post comes from something I built, broke or fixed. Where it makes sense I will include the commands, the config and the numbers, so you can try it yourself.

The source for this blog is public at [github.com/chanupen/blog](https://github.com/chanupen/blog). If you spot a mistake, open an issue or leave a comment below.
